Privacy Policy for ShopCoin15 Studio

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Introduction

ShopCoin15 Studio ("we", "our", or "us") provides content creation and automated media publishing tools for social media platforms. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you connect and publish content via our application.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect authorization tokens necessary to publish authorized video content and retrieve account identification through the official TikTok API (Login Kit & Content Posting API). We do not collect passwords or sensitive personal data.

3. How We Use Information

Authorized access tokens are solely used to upload video reels, captions, and thumbnails created inside the ShopCoin15 Studio directly to the creator's TikTok profile upon explicit user action.

4. Data Storage & Security

Tokens are stored securely in encrypted server configurations and are never shared with third parties. Users may revoke access at any time via their TikTok account security settings.

5. Contact Us

For any privacy inquiries regarding ShopCoin15 Studio, contact: support@shopcoin15.com or via Instagram @shop_coin15.
